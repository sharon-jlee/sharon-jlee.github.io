EXPERIENCE 02

National Comedy Center

As a Nonprofit Consulting Intern, I worked with the National Comedy Center to develop strategic recommendations for its Memory Café program, focusing on sustainable funding, nonprofit operations, and AI-assisted fundraising strategies.

Role

Nonprofit Consulting Intern | Summer 2026

What I Worked On

I evaluated the National Comedy Center's Memory Café, a free monthly program supporting individuals living with dementia and their caregivers through comedy and shared experiences.

My work focused on identifying opportunities to strengthen the program's long-term financial sustainability. I researched prospective foundation partners, evaluated comparable nonprofit programs, and developed recommendations for fundraising and operational improvements.

I also explored how AI tools could support donor discovery, grant research, and personalized outreach, helping a small administrative team streamline fundraising processes.

Connection to Project Work

This experience connects directly to my nonprofit consulting project, Building a Sustainable Funding Model Through AI Strategy. I developed a final presentation outlining recommendations to strengthen the Memory Café's funding model through targeted donor research, strategic partnerships, and AI-assisted fundraising workflows.

Skills Used

Nonprofit Consulting, Strategic Planning, Research, Data Analysis, AI Strategy, Fundraising Research, Project Management, Communication

What I Learned

This externship strengthened my understanding of how nonprofit organizations balance financial sustainability with their social missions. I gained experience translating research and operational insights into actionable recommendations while exploring how emerging technologies can help resource-constrained organizations work more efficiently.
