PROJECT 04

Project Overview

Worked with the National Comedy Center in Jamestown, NY, to evaluate strategic growth opportunities for its Memory Café, a free monthly program supporting individuals living with dementia and their caregivers through comedy and shared experiences.

Contributions

  • Analyzed operational challenges and identified opportunities to improve program sustainability.
  • Researched prospective foundation partners and funding opportunities focused on aging, caregiving, and arts-based wellness.
  • Evaluated comparable programs and developed recommendations for donor outreach and long-term growth.
  • Explored AI-assisted workflows to streamline grant research, donor discovery, and fundraising processes for a small administrative team.

Skills Used

Project Management, Data analysis, Consumer Analytics, Communication

Project Deliverables

Developed strategic recommendations supported by comparative research, funding analysis, and operational assessments.

Final Deliverable

Building a Sustainable Funding Model Through AI Strategy

Developed a strategic proposal for the National Comedy Center's Memory Cafe, recommending AI-assisted donor discovery, grant research, and personalized outreach strategies to support long-term funding sustainability.
